Task: say what the system is for and what matters most about it. The item gives the system's units and the people who use it, each with a sentence saying what it does (worded earlier from facts), the external systems it uses, the titles of its main flows, and what the READMEs of its repositories claim.
Write the purpose: one plain sentence of 10 to 25 words saying what the system as a whole is for, such as what it lets its users do or the business it serves. Take it from the README claims when they state it; otherwise infer it from what the units do together. Do not list the units.
Write the key fact: one plain sentence of 10 to 30 words giving the single fact about how the system is built that a new reader most needs, such as how its parts communicate, where its data lives, or what everything depends on.
Leave either out when the inputs do not show it.
