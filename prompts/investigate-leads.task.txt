Read with the tools in small, targeted calls, several independent ones in one turn when you can, asking for numbered lines. Stop as soon as the code settles the question.

Answer once, through the answer tool, as TOML in this form:

verdict = "confirmed"   # confirmed, refuted or unclear
title = "..."           # confirmed only: a title of 5 to 7 words
finding = "..."         # confirmed only: the finding, what the code does, in one or two sentences of 20 to 60 words
consequence = "..."     # confirmed only: the consequence, what can happen because of it, in one or two sentences of 22 to 60 words
impact = "medium"       # confirmed only: high, medium or low, by what an attacker or a failure could do to data or availability
confidence = "high"     # confirmed only: high, medium or low

[[evidence]]            # at least one, at most 8
lines = "Shop:orders-service/src/main/java/Orders.java:12-30"   # repo:path:from-to, at most 40 lines

A refuted verdict needs evidence that shows where the risk is stopped. An unclear verdict needs evidence or a reason = "what the code does not show". The repository is the name before the first colon, as [task] lists it; a service's folder inside a repository is part of the path. Every class, method, field, path, header, setting or topic the title, finding or consequence names must be in the shows of some evidence. Name no tool, file or technology the evidence does not show, not even to say it is missing. Before you answer, check that the title has 5 to 7 words.
